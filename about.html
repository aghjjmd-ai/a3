About Thinker

We teach the thinking behind every other subject.

Thinker is a small, independent study site. Our subject is reasoning: how to test an idea, judge where information comes from and solve a problem without panicking.

These skills sit underneath maths, science, history and everyday decisions alike. They are rarely taught directly, and they improve quickly with a little deliberate practice.

How we work

Our guides draw on established material from logic, research methods and education, written in plain language and reviewed by a second reader. Examples are invented for teaching and do not describe real people.

What we are not

We are not a school or a tutoring service, and we do not take positions on political or contested questions. Our aim is to help you think them through for yourself.
